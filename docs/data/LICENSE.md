# Syzygeon dataset

The files in this folder (`manifest.json` and `*.bin`) were computed from the
JPL DE441 ephemeris with `alignments.py` and `export_web.py`.

They are licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License (CC BY 4.0)**: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you give credit, for
example: *"Planetary alignment data from Syzygeon, computed with JPL DE441."*

The underlying ephemeris, JPL DE441 (Park et al. 2021, *The Astronomical
Journal* 161:105), is distributed freely by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
